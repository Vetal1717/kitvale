Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Kitvale Sheets is used only by the owner of the Kitvale shop. It asks Google for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files that the tool itself created.

What data is used

The tool uploads the shop's own spreadsheet templates to the owner's Google Drive and sets them to view-only sharing, so buyers can make a copy. No other Google Drive files are accessed.

What is not collected

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of buyers or other users. Buyers who open a shared sheet interact with Google Sheets directly, under Google's own privacy policy.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
